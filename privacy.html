Privacy Policy

Rebond Studio · Last updated November 3, 2026

Rebond Studio only processes the data needed to send drafts to and read statistics from the owner's own TikTok account.

  • Data received from TikTok: an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID, plus the public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), obtained with the user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list and video.upload scopes.
  • Use: uploading video drafts to the owner's inbox and measuring how the owner's own videos perform. No data about other users is collected.
  • Storage: data is kept in the owner's private workspace and is never shared or sold.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
